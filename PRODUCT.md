# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Kompak serves residents and RT/RW community administrators. Residents use the mobile app to follow neighborhood activities, attendance, points, rewards, and announcements. Administrators use focused operational surfaces to review and manage resident records while working from a phone.

## Product Purpose

Kompak brings everyday neighborhood administration and participation into one clear mobile experience. Success means residents can understand what is happening and take part, while administrators can find records, recognize account state, and take routine actions quickly and confidently.

## Brand Personality

Practical, trustworthy, and community-focused. The interface should feel welcoming without becoming playful, and operational without feeling corporate or bureaucratic.

## Anti-references

Avoid ornamental dashboards, dense enterprise admin software, decorative data visualization, and unfamiliar controls. Do not make routine neighborhood tasks feel technical, financial, or institutional.

## Design Principles

- Put the next community task ahead of decoration.
- Make identity, status, and available actions unmistakable at a glance.
- Use familiar mobile patterns so occasional administrators need little training.
- Keep resident and administrator experiences coherent while exposing only role-appropriate controls.
- Use plain Indonesian labels that describe recognizable community tasks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, support system text scaling, provide meaningful semantic labels and touch targets, never rely on color alone for status, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
